# Control 2.4: Information Barriers for Copilot (Chinese Wall)

**Control ID:** 2.4
**Pillar:** Security & Protection
**Regulatory Reference:** SEC Rule 10b-5, FINRA 5280, Chinese Wall Requirements
**Last Verified:** 2026-09-24
**Regulatory Applicability:** Firm/counsel determination — see [Regulatory Applicability Questions](../../reference/regulatory-applicability-questions.md)
**Governance Levels:** Baseline / Recommended / Regulated

---

## Objective

Deploy Microsoft Purview Information Barriers (IB) to restrict communication and collaboration between organizational segments in the Microsoft 365 workloads where Microsoft documents IB enforcement, and validate that Microsoft Copilot grounding respects those boundaries for the firm's approved use cases. This control is intended for firms whose legal and compliance owners determine that ethical wall / Chinese Wall controls are required for scenarios such as investment banking and research separation, proprietary trading and client advisory separation, or merging entities during M&A transactions.

---

## Why This Matters for FSI

- **SEC Rule 10b-5:** Legal/compliance should determine whether a Copilot use case creates MNPI misuse or antifraud risk that requires information barrier controls and testing.
- **FINRA Rule 5280:** Broker-dealer compliance should determine whether trading-ahead-of-research controls apply to the firm's Copilot-assisted research or trading workflows.
- **FINRA Rule 2241 (Equity Research)** and **Rule 2242 (Debt Research):** Legal/compliance should determine which research/investment-banking separation requirements apply to the firm's Copilot-enabled workflows.
- **SEC Regulation AC:** Legal/compliance should determine whether Copilot-assisted analyst workflows require additional independence, certification, or supervision controls.
- **Chinese Wall / ethical wall policies:** Where firm policy requires separation of private-side/public-side information, Copilot's cross-workload retrieval should be included in barrier design and effectiveness testing.

## Control Description

Microsoft Purview Information Barriers restrict communication and collaboration between defined user segments in Microsoft Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Planner, and related supported collaboration experiences. Microsoft Learn also documents that IB policies cannot restrict email communication in Exchange Online. For Copilot, treat IB as an underlying workload boundary that must be validated in each tenant and surface:

1. **SharePoint and OneDrive:** IB policies can prevent unauthorized site/content access, sharing, and search; test Copilot grounding against segment-exclusive test content.
2. **Teams:** IB policies prevent unauthorized communication and collaboration activities; test Teams Copilot scenarios only after Teams IB policy application is active.
3. **Exchange Online:** IB policies do not restrict email communication; use Exchange mail flow rules or other approved controls where email separation is required.
4. **Unsupported/limited Copilot surfaces:** Channel Agent in Teams does not support IB. Copilot Pages and Copilot Notebooks use SharePoint Embedded content where Information Barriers are not supported.

### IB Mode and Segment Membership

Microsoft Purview Information Barriers operates in one of three modes that determine how many segments a user may belong to:

| IB Mode | Segment Limit per User | Total Segments Supported | Notes |
|---------|----------------------|--------------------------|-------|
| **Legacy** | 1 (exactly one segment) | 250 | Oldest mode; users cannot be assigned to multiple segments; upgrade path available |
| **SingleSegment** (non-Legacy) | 1 | 5,000 | Current non-Legacy baseline; supports multi-segment upgrade |
| **MultiSegment** (non-Legacy) | Up to 10 | 5,000 | Current recommended mode; enables complex organizational scenarios |

**Multi-segment support** (non-Legacy MultiSegment mode) allows users to belong to up to 10 IB segments simultaneously. User compatibility is determined by shared segment membership — if two users share at least one common segment, they are compatible under IB policy. This enables scenarios such as Compliance personnel who span multiple business units, deal team members who temporarily acquire an additional segment during a transaction, or supervisory users who need access across segments without full policy override.

**FSI implication:** Organizations should verify their current IB mode by running `Get-PolicyConfig` and checking the `InformationBarrierMode` property. Legacy-mode organizations should plan an upgrade path, as Legacy mode limits segment count to 250 and restricts each user to one segment — both constraints that create operational challenges for large diversified financial institutions. Organizations should verify that their Copilot-related IB configurations and compensating controls account for their specific IB mode behavior.

### Barrier Types and Copilot Impact

| Barrier Type | Duration | Copilot Impact | Example |
|-------------|----------|----------------|---------|
| **Permanent** | Ongoing | Supported workload barriers should prevent cross-segment SharePoint/OneDrive/Teams content access; Copilot grounding must be tenant-tested against those sources | Research vs. Investment Banking |
| **Temporary (Deal-Specific)** | Duration of transaction | Additional barrier restricting deal team content from non-deal personnel where the underlying workload supports IB | M&A target information |
| **Situational** | Activated/deactivated as needed | Client advisory restricted from specific IB deal information where the underlying workload supports IB | Wealth client also an M&A target |
| **Supervisory** | Ongoing with override | Compliance can access all segments for supervisory purposes | Compliance monitoring |

### Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat Cross-Workload Search Implications

Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat performs a cross-workload search when answering user queries. Without information barriers, a single Copilot Chat prompt could retrieve:

```

         │

    ├──────────────────────────────────────────┤
    │ SharePoint: M&A deal room documents (IB) │

    │ Teams: Trading desk conversations         │
    │ OneDrive: Client advisory presentations   │

    ┌────┴────────────────────────────────────┐
    │            With Barriers                 │

    │ segment-accessible + non-restricted data │
```

### Channel Agent IB Limitation and Compensating Controls

**Channel Agent in Teams does not support Information Barriers.** This is a documented platform limitation: unlike standard Copilot Chat and Teams Copilot features, Channel Agent is not subject to IB policy enforcement and may return content that crosses information barrier boundaries.

**FSI regulatory impact:** If firm counsel/compliance determines that SEC Rule 10b-5, FINRA Rules 5280/2241/2242, or firm ethical wall policies apply to the use case, a Channel Agent deployed in a Teams channel that includes members from barrier-separated segments (e.g., Investment Banking and Research) could surface barrier-restricted information without IB enforcement. Document the limitation, approved deployment criteria, and escalation process in supervisory procedures and information barrier policies.

**Required compensating controls:**

1. **Restrict Channel Agent deployment to homogeneous segments.** Do not deploy Channel Agent in any Teams channel where members from IB-separated segments are present. Before deploying a Channel Agent, audit the channel's membership to confirm all members belong to a single IB segment or are in non-restricted segments.

2. **Do not rely on sensitivity-label DLP to block Channel Agent.** Microsoft Learn documents that Channel Agent in Teams cannot be prevented from summarizing labeled files that are identified by a Microsoft Purview DLP policy. Sensitivity labels remain useful for classification, label inheritance, and EXTRACT-rights behavior for the requesting user, but they are not a compensating control for IB enforcement in Channel Agent.

3. **Monitor Channel Agent activity where tenant telemetry supports it.** Verify in your tenant whether DSPM, Purview audit, Teams app inventory, and agent registry views expose Channel Agent deployments and interactions. Anomalous cross-segment content surfacing via Channel Agent should generate compliance review under the firm's incident process.

4. **Document in supervisory procedures.** The Channel Agent IB limitation and the firm's compensating controls must be documented in supervisory procedures, information barrier policies, and (for Regulated level) in the regulatory examination package.

## Setup & Configuration

### Step 1: Define User Segments

**Portal:** Microsoft Purview > Information barriers > Segments

1. Create segments based on Entra ID user attributes (typically Department or custom attributes)
2. Ensure all Copilot-licensed users are assigned to at least one segment. In non-Legacy IB mode (SingleSegment or MultiSegment), users may be assigned to **up to 10 segments** — this multi-segment support enables more complex organizational scenarios such as Compliance users spanning multiple business units or M&A team members needing temporary additional segment assignments. In Legacy mode, each user is restricted to exactly one segment (maximum 250 total segments). Organizations should verify their IB mode by running `Get-PolicyConfig` and checking the `InformationBarrierMode` property.
3. Verify segment membership accuracy with business stakeholders

```powershell
# Connect to Security & Compliance PowerShell
Connect-IPPSSession

# Create segment definitions
New-OrganizationSegment -Name "InvestmentBanking" `
  -UserGroupFilter "Department -eq 'Investment Banking'"

New-OrganizationSegment -Name "EquityResearch" `
  -UserGroupFilter "Department -eq 'Equity Research'"

New-OrganizationSegment -Name "Trading" `
  -UserGroupFilter "Department -eq 'Proprietary Trading'"
```

### Step 2: Create Information Barrier Policies

```powershell
# Block Investment Banking from Research
New-InformationBarrierPolicy -Name "IB-Research-Block" `
  -AssignedSegment "InvestmentBanking" `
  -SegmentsBlocked "EquityResearch","DebtResearch" `
  -State Active
# IMPORTANT: Create the reciprocal policy (Research blocked from InvestmentBanking) to ensure bidirectional enforcement

# Block Trading from Research
New-InformationBarrierPolicy -Name "Trading-Research-Block" `
  -AssignedSegment "Trading" `
  -SegmentsBlocked "EquityResearch","DebtResearch" `
  -State Active
# IMPORTANT: Create the reciprocal policy (Research blocked from Trading) to ensure bidirectional enforcement

# Block Investment Banking from Trading
New-InformationBarrierPolicy -Name "IB-Trading-Block" `
  -AssignedSegment "InvestmentBanking" `
  -SegmentsBlocked "Trading" `
  -State Active
# IMPORTANT: Create the reciprocal policy (Trading blocked from InvestmentBanking) to ensure bidirectional enforcement
```

### Step 3: Apply Barrier Policies

```powershell
# Start policy application (this processes all policies)
Start-InformationBarrierPoliciesApplication

# Check application status
Get-InformationBarrierPoliciesApplicationStatus
```

### Step 4: Enable Information Barriers for SharePoint and OneDrive

**Portal:** SharePoint admin center > Settings > Information barriers

1. Enable information barriers for SharePoint
2. Enable information barriers for OneDrive
3. Apply IB mode to sites: "Owner Moderated" or "Implicit" based on firm requirements

### Step 5: Verify Copilot Barrier Enforcement

1. Sign in as an Investment Banking user
2. Open Copilot Chat and query for content that should be restricted (e.g., research reports)
3. Verify that Copilot does not return results from barrier-separated segments
4. Repeat for other segment combinations

### Step 6: Configure Deal-Specific Temporary Barriers

```powershell
# Create temporary deal team segment
New-OrganizationSegment -Name "ProjectAlpha-DealTeam" `
  -UserGroupFilter "CustomAttribute1 -eq 'ProjectAlpha'"

# Create barrier blocking non-deal personnel
New-InformationBarrierPolicy -Name "ProjectAlpha-Barrier" `
  -AssignedSegment "ProjectAlpha-DealTeam" `
  -SegmentsBlocked "ClientAdvisory","RetailBanking" `
  -State Active
```

## Financial Sector Considerations

- **Broker-Dealer Operations:** Where broker-dealer compliance determines that investment banking / research separation applies, Copilot test plans should verify that approved AI surfaces do not expose segment-exclusive content across the firm's wall.
- **Multi-Service Financial Institutions:** Firms operating across banking, insurance, and securities face the most complex barrier requirements. Each line of business may require separation from multiple other segments, creating a matrix of barrier policies.
- **Deal-Specific Barriers:** M&A transactions require temporary barriers that restrict deal information to authorized deal team members. These barriers must be provisioned rapidly (within hours of deal engagement) and decommissioned cleanly after transaction close. Copilot must respect these temporary barriers immediately upon activation.
- **Supervisory Access:** Compliance and legal functions typically need supervisory access across all segments for monitoring purposes. Information barriers support this through allow policies that permit one-way or bidirectional access for supervisory segments. Document the business justification for all supervisory access exceptions.
- **Wall-Crossing Procedures:** When employees are temporarily "brought over the wall" for a transaction, their information barrier segment assignment must be updated and Copilot access adjusted accordingly. Establish a documented wall-crossing procedure that includes Copilot access considerations.
- **Private-Side vs. Public-Side:** Financial firms distinguish between private-side (access to MNPI) and public-side (no MNPI access) roles. Information barriers should align precisely with the firm's private-side/public-side designation. Any misalignment creates regulatory risk.
- **Examination History:** SEC and FINRA have brought enforcement actions against firms with inadequate information barriers. AI tools that access data across organizational boundaries represent a heightened enforcement risk. Maintain detailed evidence of barrier effectiveness testing.
- **Work IQ and cross-app grounding:** M365 roadmap items show Work IQ features for PowerPoint and Work IQ APIs reaching GA in 2026. For FSI organizations with ethical-wall requirements, any Copilot feature that can reason across files, meetings, emails, or agents should be included in tenant functional tests to confirm whether segment boundaries are respected for the approved workflow.

## Verification Criteria

1. **Segment Assignment:** Verify that all Copilot-licensed users are assigned to at least one appropriate organizational segment; confirm IB mode (`Get-PolicyConfig`) and that multi-segment assignments, if used, are intentional and documented
2. **Policy Application Status:** Confirm all information barrier policies show "Active" and "Applied" status
3. **Copilot Chat Barrier Enforcement:** As an Investment Banking user, query Copilot Chat for research content — confirm no cross-segment results are returned
4. **File Grounding Restriction:** Attempt to reference a Research segment file in Copilot while signed in as an Investment Banking user — confirm Copilot cannot access the file
5. **Teams Copilot Barrier Enforcement:** Verify that Teams Copilot meeting summaries do not include content from barrier-separated participants
6. **Channel Agent IB Gap Documentation:** Confirm that Channel Agent deployments are documented with IB segment membership audits for each channel; confirm no Channel Agents are deployed in channels with mixed IB-segment membership; confirm the Channel Agent IB limitation is documented in supervisory procedures
7. **Channel Agent Compensating Controls:** Verify Channel Agent is disabled or restricted to homogeneous-segment channels; confirm the firm does not rely on sensitivity-label DLP as an IB substitute; confirm tenant telemetry monitoring is configured where available
8. **SharePoint IB Mode:** Confirm SharePoint sites have appropriate IB modes applied and that Copilot in SharePoint respects barrier boundaries
9. **Temporary Barrier Activation:** Create a test temporary barrier and verify Copilot enforcement within 1 hour of policy application
10. **Supervisory Access:** Verify that Compliance segment users can access content across barriers as designed, and that access is logged
11. **Barrier Event Audit Logs:** Confirm that information barrier policy match events appear in the Unified Audit Log
12. **Periodic Validation:** Confirm quarterly barrier validation testing is scheduled, documented, and reviewed by compliance
